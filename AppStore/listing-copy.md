## Description (max 4000 chars)

TropiFish turns your water test results into something you can actually act on. Instead of just storing numbers, it tells you what they mean for your fish and what to do next — built by tropical-fish keepers, for tropical-fish keepers.

Everything runs entirely on your device. No account, no sign-up, no internet required, and nothing about your tanks ever leaves your phone.

LOG A TEST IN UNDER A MINUTE
• Enter all seven parameters — ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, pH, KH, GH and temperature — using the same increments as your test kit.
• Log water changes separately so you can see how they affect your nitrate trend.

KNOW WHAT IT MEANS
• A clear traffic-light status for each tank, at a glance.
• Real un-ionised ammonia (NH₃) toxicity, calculated from your pH and temperature — not just the raw kit number, because the same reading can be harmless or dangerous depending on your water.
• Safe ranges tuned to your tank type, from a shrimp nano to an African cichlid setup.

KNOW WHAT TO DO
• Plain-English guidance that catches slow trends early and tells you the next step.
• Cycling-aware: a new tank's ammonia guidance stays informational instead of alarming.

STOCK THE RIGHT FISH
• A built-in guide to 130+ freshwater species with photos and practical care notes.
• A compatibility check that flags anything that won't suit your water, tank size or existing fish — before you buy.
• A stocking-level estimate so you don't overload the tank.

STAY ON TOP OF MAINTENANCE
• Track each piece of filter media and get a reminder when it's due for a change.
• Water-change reminders on a schedule you choose.

MADE FOR REAL FISHKEEPERS
TropiFish is a guide to use alongside your own judgement, not a replacement for it. Spotted something you'd change, or want a species added? There's a "Get in touch" link right in the app — we read every message.

No ads. No subscriptions. No tracking. Just a tool that helps you keep your fish healthy.
