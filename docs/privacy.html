Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 July 2026

TropiFish does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

The short version: No account. No sign-up. No analytics. No ads. No tracking. No servers. The app works entirely offline, and nothing about you or your tanks ever leaves your phone.

Information we collect

None. TropiFish has no user accounts and no backend. The data you create — your tanks, water test readings, water changes, stocked species, and filter-maintenance records — is stored only in the app's private storage on your own device.

How your data is used

Your data is used solely to provide the app's features on your device: showing your tank status, calculating guidance, drawing trend charts, and scheduling local reminders. It is never uploaded, sold, or shared with anyone.

Notifications

If you enable water-change or filter-maintenance reminders, TropiFish schedules local notifications on your device. These are generated and delivered entirely on-device and are not sent through any external service.

Third parties

TropiFish contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and makes no network requests. The bundled fish photographs are licensed from Wikimedia Commons and are included with the app; loading them involves no network activity and no data about you.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you are always in full control of it. Deleting a tank removes its records, and deleting the app removes all of its data permanently. We hold nothing to delete on our side, because we never receive anything.

Children's privacy

TropiFish is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
