Common questions

How do I log a water test?
Open a tank and tap Log Test. Enter whichever parameters you measured — you can skip any you didn't test — and save. Your tank's traffic-light status and guidance update straight away.
Why does the app show a different ammonia value than my kit?
Your kit measures total ammonia. TropiFish also calculates the toxic portion (un-ionised NH₃) from your pH and temperature, because the same reading can be harmless or dangerous depending on your water. Log ammonia, pH and temperature together to see it.
Do reminders work if I'm offline?
Yes. All reminders are scheduled locally on your device and need no internet connection. Make sure notifications are allowed for TropiFish in iOS Settings.
Is my data backed up or synced?
Your data is stored privately on your device and is included in your normal encrypted iPhone/iCloud device backup. TropiFish itself has no servers and never uploads your data. Deleting the app removes its data permanently.
How accurate is the fish guide?
The species care ranges and compatibility checks are sensible, well-researched starting points — but TropiFish is a guide to use alongside your own judgement, not a substitute for it. Always cross-check anything important for your fish, and tell us if you spot something that looks off.
Which devices are supported?
iPhone and iPad running a recent version of iOS/iPadOS.
